how to play

  1. 01guess

    Type any word from the dictionary. Each valid guess gets a rank.

  2. 02rank

    Rank 1 is the secret word. The lower the rank, the closer your word's meaning, measured by cosine similarity between word vectors.

  3. 03hint

    Hint 1 shows how many letters the word has, hint 2 reveals the first letter, hint 3 the last letter. After that, each hint reveals a word ranked closer than your best guess. It counts as a guess and is outlined in gold. Once you are inside the top 50, no more hints.

  4. 04give up

    Reveals the secret word and the 20 words closest to it.
